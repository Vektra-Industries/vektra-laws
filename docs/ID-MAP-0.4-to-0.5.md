# Clause id map: 0.4.2 to 0.5.0

*This document is informative.*

Version 0.5.0 replaces the five Laws of 0.4.2 (The Mission, Sustain, Hold, Finish, Ready) with a
preamble (the Mission) and five new Laws (Protect, Free, True, Serve, Stand), approved by the owner
on 2026-11-21. Every 0.4.2 clause was kept and moved to a new Law, except where two clauses said the
same thing; those were merged and are marked below. Because ids now have new meanings, a
declaration, a results file or a guard that cites 0.4.2 ids keeps citing version 0.4.2, or is
updated with this map. The renumbering was approved by the owner before version 1.0; see the
stability rules in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md).

## Laws

| 0.4.2 | 0.5.0 |
|---|---|
| L1 The Mission | Preamble: The Mission (not a numbered Law); clauses P.1 to P.3 |
| L2 Sustain | Mostly Law 1 Protect; limits, data and copying itself to Law 4 Serve; oversight and power concentration to Law 2 Free; checking claims to Law 3 True |
| L3 Hold | Law 2 Free; outside text to Law 4 Serve; plain speech and honesty to Law 3 True |
| L4 Finish | Law 3 True (proof, claims, records); task status to Law 5 Stand; the open objective and self-checks to Law 4 Serve |
| L5 Ready | Law 5 Stand; the record to Law 3 True |

## Conformance cases

Case ids did not change. Every case cites the new clause ids, and each case now sits under the
heading of the Law of its first cited clause, except the cases for the Understanding Matrix, which sit under their own heading. Cases AG-46 to AG-84, RB-20 to RB-22, MD-09, MD-10,
OP-09 and OP-10 are new.
