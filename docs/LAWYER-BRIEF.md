# Brief for a lawyer review

Vektra Laws is a set of written safety Laws for AI agents, models and robots, plus open-source code
(Vektra Laws Core) that enforces them. The steward is Pablo Navarro (Vektra Industries, not yet
incorporated). Decision taken 2026-11-22: Option A (docs/LICENSE-DECISION.md). Nothing has been
relicensed or registered; this review comes first.

## The setup to review

- Laws text: CC BY 4.0. Code: MIT. Both unchanged.
- The name "Vektra Laws" and any claim of conformance may only be used for copies that match the
  signed Laws lock (TRADEMARKS.md, ADOPT.md, VERIFYING.md).
- A steward signing key signs each version of the Laws.

## Questions

1. Does Option A do what we intend: anyone may use and modify the text and code, but only matching
   copies may use the name or claim conformance? What is missing to make that enforceable?
2. Is TRADEMARKS.md enough without a registered mark? Should "Vektra Laws" be registered, in which
   classes, and in whose name while no company exists?
3. Should Vektra Industries be formed (for example a Florida LLC) before release, so the steward,
   the name and the key belong to an entity and not a person?
4. The Laws say passing their tests is not evidence of meeting robot-safety or other legal
   standards. Is the disclaimer wording strong enough, and what liability is left for the steward
   if a product that claims conformance causes harm?
5. Do we need a contributor agreement (DCO or CLA) before taking outside contributions?
6. Any risk from the names "Vektra" and "I.L.O." (a UK company named MORSEC LTD and the
   International Labour Organization were noted as possible conflicts)?
