# Open questions

Only the questions that need a decision. Each one says what the current draft does.

## Needs the owner (before v0.2 is approved)

1. **New one-line statements for Law 1 and Law 3.** The approved v2 lines named Vektra and used
   the word "harness" and the idiom "takes the wheel", which the universal rules do not allow.
   This draft uses:
   - Law 1: "Serve Earth, humanity and every world we reach." (was "Be a harness for Earth, for
     humanity and for every world we reach.")
   - Law 3: "People lead; the system supports and never takes control." (was "People lead;
     Vektra supports. The harness never takes the wheel.")
   - Law 2 and Law 4 were also made plainer: "Protect and support the people you serve." (was
     "hold up", which can also mean "rob") and "Complete the mission honestly; leave no work
     unfinished." (was "leave nothing half-done").
   Approve, or give other wording.
2. **The Vektra Foundation.** GOVERNANCE.md names the Vektra Foundation as steward and Vektra as
   owner. The copyright line in the licenses says "Vektra Industries (Pablo Navarro)". Confirm
   the legal names to use in each place.
3. **Action level definitions.** The draft keeps the levels from the brief (0 READ, 1 PREPARE,
   2 REVERSIBLE WRITE, 3 EXTERNAL ACTION, 4 MONEY OR IRREVERSIBLE). Ada proposed different
   definitions for levels 1 to 4. Keep the current levels, or switch?

## Needs a reviewer (by the version named)

4. **Spanish translation (v0.2).** A native speaker should review it, especially the Law names
   "Respaldar" (Hold), "Cumplir" (Finish) and "Listos" (Ready).
5. **Robots in shared space (v0.3).** Any motion in a space shared with people is level 3, so each
   such action needs a person's approval. That is not practical for a robot that works among
   people. Options: an approved per-mission scope that lists specific motions, or a stricter
   level 2 for low-force motion inside tested limits.
6. **Record signing (v1.0).** 5.4 says the record SHOULD be signed or keyed. Raise it to MUST by
   v1.0?

## Resolved

- **Ethos word** (2026-10-04): Sustain, confirmed by Pablo.
- **Who may claim conformance** (v0.2): three levels, Adopts, Tested and Verified, by
  self-declaration with published evidence; Verified needs an independent reviewer (ADOPT.md).
- **Protection of the name** (v0.2): the README states that the license covers the text, not the
  names "Vektra" and "Vektra Laws". A formal trademark policy can come later if needed.
