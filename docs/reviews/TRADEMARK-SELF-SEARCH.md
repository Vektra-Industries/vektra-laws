# Trademark self-search: VEKTRA INDUSTRIES and related marks

*This document is informative.*

> **Read this first.** This is a free self-search done by the project on 2026-10-04 (ET), without
> a lawyer. It is **not legal advice**, not a clearance opinion and not a full search. It covers
> only the free sources listed below. Common-law (unregistered) use, state registrations and
> many national registers were not searched. A trademark attorney should run a full search
> before any filing or launch, when funding allows.

## 1. Scope

- **Primary subject:** VEKTRA INDUSTRIES, used as the house mark and the name of the
  organization. Also VEKTRA LAWS (the name of the standard) and VEKTRA FOUNDATION (the named
  steward).
- **Related-marks context:** VEKTRA and VECTRA alone, because VEKTRA is the dominant element of
  every name above and VECTRA sounds the same.
- **Classes of interest (Nice):** 9 (software, downloadable publications), 35 (business
  services), 41 (education, publishing), 42 (software and technology services) and 45 (legal and
  licensing services). Live, dead and pending records were included.

## 4. Likelihood-of-confusion read

The usual factors include the similarity of the marks (sight, sound, meaning), the relatedness of
the goods and services, and the channels of trade. VEKTRA and VECTRA sound the same, and they
look alike. "Industries", "Laws" and "Foundation" are descriptive or generic words and add little
distinction. The read below is a self-assessment only.

| Use | United States | EU and UK | Main reasons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VEKTRA INDUSTRIES as house mark and organization name** (trade name; corporate identity across AI, software, robotics, communications) | **Medium** | **Medium** | No identical mark. The dominant word matches VECTRA (Vectra AI, Inc.: AI and network-security software, classes 9 and 42) and the pending VEKTRA application (telematics software, 35 and 42). A trade name alone, used with distinct product names, carries less risk than VEKTRA used on software. |
| **Division name "Vektra AI"** | **High** | Medium | Close to the registrant's company name, Vectra AI, Inc., in the same field (AI software). Sound is identical. |
| **VEKTRA LAWS** (name of a free, voluntary standard: publications, class 9, 16 or 41) | **Low** | Low to medium | The goods (a standards text) differ from security software, telematics or emergency systems. The risk rises if the name is used for software, tools or services, or for emergency and communications systems in Europe. |
| **VEKTRA FOUNDATION** (steward) | Low | Low | No similar foundation mark found. It would rise if the foundation offered software or certification services. |
| **Software products branded VEKTRA or VEKTRA + generic word** (classes 9 and 42) | **High** | **High** | US: Vectra AI registration (incontestable status acknowledged, renewed) and the pending VEKTRA SaaS application, which was filed before any project filing. EU and UK: Telegrafia's Vektra registration for emergency warning and communication software, which is close to the communications and disaster-response mission. |

## 5. Options (not recommendations; no spend is recommended in this document)

1. **Use VEKTRA INDUSTRIES as a house mark and trade name only**, next to distinct product names
   (as with the existing product names), and avoid VEKTRA alone or "Vektra + generic word" as a
   product mark in classes 9 and 42.
2. **Rename the "Vektra AI" division** to a name that does not combine VEKTRA with "AI", or
   describe it in words ("the AI division of Vektra Industries").
3. **Keep the name of the standard but add a non-affiliation line** in informative text, for
   example: "Vektra Laws is not affiliated with any owner of a VECTRA or VEKTRA trademark." A
   disclaimer lowers the chance of actual confusion; it does not cure a likelihood of confusion.
4. **Give the standard its own distinctive name** (with "by Vektra Industries" as attribution),
   which separates the standard from the house mark and lowers risk for both.
5. **If a filing is made later**, consider the less crowded classes first (for example class 41
   for publishing and educational services related to a standard, or class 45), and file for
   VEKTRA INDUSTRIES as a whole. A USPTO base application costs $350 per class (see
   LEGAL-SELF-REVIEW.md). An attorney knockout search should come first.
6. **Watch the pending VEKTRA application (SN 99823105)**. If it is published for opposition, a
   30-day window opens for anyone who believes they would be damaged. Whether to act depends on
   the project's own date of first use and on advice.
7. **For Europe and the UK**, avoid the VEKTRA name on emergency warning, notification or
   communications software until an EUIPO and UK search and advice are done.
