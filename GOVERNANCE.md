# Governance

## Roles

- **Owner.** Vektra owns the Vektra Laws, the names "Vektra" and "Vektra Laws", and this
  repository. Ownership stays with Vektra.
- **Steward.** The Vektra Foundation keeps the Laws on Vektra's behalf. The steward maintains the
  reference text, runs the review process, publishes versions, and keeps the list of reference
  adopters and translations.
- **Adopters.** Anyone may adopt the Laws for any agent, robot or model, from any maker, in any
  country, free of charge. Adopters do not need permission. They declare a conformance level with
  [ADOPT.md](ADOPT.md).
- **Contributors.** Anyone may propose a change, a test case, a translation or a new profile.
- **Reviewers.** People who review proposals in their field (for example robot safety, model
  evaluation, law, or a language). The steward invites reviewers and lists them in each release.

## Open review process

1. **Propose.** Open an issue or a pull request. State the problem, the clause ids it affects and
   the evidence (for example a failure case).
2. **Discuss.** The proposal stays open for public comment. Larger changes stay open longer.
3. **Review.** At least one reviewer in the relevant field reviews the proposal. A translation is
   reviewed by a fluent speaker of that language.
4. **Decide.** The steward accepts, asks for changes, or declines, and gives the reason in public.
5. **Publish.** Accepted changes go into the next version, with credit in the CHANGELOG.

## What needs the owner's approval

- Any change to the five Laws, their order, their one-line statements or the motto.
- Any change to the approval rule (3.5) or the call-off clause (3.9).
- Any change to this governance document.

## Stability rules

- The English text is the reference text. Translations follow it.
- A published clause id is never reused with a different meaning. A removed clause is marked
  retired, not renumbered.
- A profile can add clauses that are stricter than the core, never weaker.
- The conformance suite is versioned with the Laws. Every conformance claim names both versions.

## Status of this process

This is the v0.2 draft. The process opens to the public with v1.0 (see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)).
Until then, the steward runs reviews with invited reviewers.
